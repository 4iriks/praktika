# Video checklist

- [ ] Live services stable; import/index jobs terminal; acceptance freshly generated.
- [ ] 1920×1080, no DevTools, notifications or personal data.
- [ ] Demo accounts only; no `.env`, cookies, API keys or logs with bodies.
- [ ] Queries rehearsed but results are live and identified as such.
- [ ] Show BM25, Vector, Hybrid, filters, scores, RAG, citations and refusal.
- [ ] Show USER/EDITOR/ADMIN boundaries, job progress, audit and system status.
- [ ] Do not perform full sync, destructive cleanup or restore.
- [ ] Capture a short fallback recording and label its timestamp/index version.
